# Local Recommendations

## Purpose
Help guests choose among San Vicente's main activity zones without mixing locations or travel times.

## Core Location Rules
- Treat BAIA/Panindigan, Poblacion, Long Beach, Alimanguan, Port Barton, northern San Vicente, and remote southern areas as distinct travel zones.
- Explain that Port Barton is close across the bay but separated by a mountain ridge for land travel.
- Do not present road and boat travel times as guaranteed; they depend on weather, sea conditions, route, and transport availability.

## Recommendation Frame
When a guest asks what to do, start with:

"Would you prefer a relaxed island-hopping day with turtles in Port Barton Bay, a morning jungle-waterfall trek at Pamuayan or Bigaho, or a sunset surf session in Alimanguan?"

## Procedure
1. Ask where the guest is starting from.
2. Ask how much time they have.
3. Ask whether they prefer water, hiking, surfing, food, or sunset.
4. Give no more than three options.
5. Include the travel implication for each option.
6. State which details require a live check.

## Never Guarantee
- Turtle sightings
- Waterfall flow
- Surf conditions
- Boat departure times
- Weather
- Road conditions
- Tide
- Opening hours
- Tour itinerary
